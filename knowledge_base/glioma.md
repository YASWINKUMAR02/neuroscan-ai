# Clinical Guidelines & Pathology: Glioma (WHO CNS 5 Classification)

## 1. Overview & Classification
Gliomas are tumors originating from glial cells (astrocytes, oligodendrocytes, and ependymal cells) in the brain or spinal cord. The 2021 WHO Classification of Tumors of the Central Nervous System (WHO CNS 5) integrates molecular parameters with histological features:

### Adult-Type Diffuse Gliomas:
1. **Astrocytoma, IDH-mutant** (WHO CNS Grades 2, 3, 4):
   - Defined by IDH1 (R132H) or IDH2 mutations without 1p/19q codeletion.
   - Grade 4 designated by presence of CDKN2A/B homozygous deletion even in the absence of microvascular proliferation or necrosis.
2. **Oligodendroglioma, IDH-mutant and 1p/19q-codeleted** (WHO CNS Grades 2, 3):
   - Requires combined IDH mutation and whole-arm 1p/19q codeletion.
   - Favorable prognosis and high chemosensitivity to PCV/TMZ regimens.
3. **Glioblastoma (GBM), IDH-wildtype** (WHO CNS Grade 4):
   - Most aggressive primary brain tumor.
   - Defined by IDH-wildtype status plus at least one of: microvascular proliferation, necrosis, TERT promoter mutation, EGFR gene amplification, or +7/-10 chromosome copy number changes.

## 2. Standard Clinical Treatment Protocols

### Maximal Safe Surgical Resection:
- The primary goal is gross total resection (GTR) of the contrast-enhancing tumor mass while preserving eloquent neurological function.
- Intraoperative adjuncts recommended: 5-ALA fluorescence-guided surgery, intraoperative MRI (iMRI), and awake cortical mapping for tumors near language or motor pathways.

### First-Line Adjuvant Therapy (Stupp Protocol for Glioblastoma):
1. **Concomitant Chemoradiotherapy**:
   - Fractionated external beam radiotherapy (60 Gy in 30 fractions over 6 weeks).
   - Concurrent oral Temozolomide (TMZ) at 75 mg/m²/day given 7 days/week during radiation.
2. **Maintenance Temozolomide**:
   - 6 cycles of adjuvant TMZ (150–200 mg/m²/day for 5 days every 28 days).
   - Addition of Tumor Treating Fields (TTFields) shows significant overall survival benefit.

---

## 3. Essential Molecular Diagnostic Tests
- **IDH1/IDH2 Mutation Testing**: R132H immunohistochemistry (IHC) followed by DNA sequencing if IHC is negative in patients under 55.
- **MGMT Promoter Methylation**: Predicts response to alkylating chemotherapy (Temozolomide).
- **1p/19q Codeletion**: FISH or chromosomal microarray testing.
- **ATRX & p53 Status**: IHC to distinguish astrocytic lineage from oligodendroglial lineage.

---

## 4. Patient Guidance & Symptoms
- **Common Symptoms**: Morning headaches, focal or generalized seizures, progressive weakness, cognitive changes, visual disturbances.
- **Seizure Management**: Levetiracetam (Keppra) or Lacosamide as first-line anti-epileptic drugs (AEDs).
- **Cerebral Edema Management**: Dexamethasone tapered to the lowest effective dose with H2 blocker/PPI gastroprotection.
