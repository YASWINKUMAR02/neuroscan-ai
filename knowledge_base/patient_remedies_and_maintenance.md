# Patient Guide: Brain Health Maintenance, Lifestyle & Supportive Care

## 1. General Brain Health & Daily Maintenance
- **Anti-Inflammatory Nutrition**: Focus on antioxidant-rich whole foods including blueberries, dark leafy greens (spinach, kale), walnuts, chia seeds, and wild salmon rich in Omega-3 fatty acids. Limit ultra-processed foods and refined sugars.
- **Hydration**: Drink 2 to 2.5 liters of clean water daily to assist cerebral perfusion, reduce tension headaches, and flush out metabolic byproducts.
- **Restful Sleep Hygiene**: Aim for 7–9 hours of deep sleep in a dark, cool room. Brain glymphatic clearance operates primarily during deep REM and slow-wave sleep.
- **Gentle Physical Activity**: Engage in daily low-impact exercise such as 20–30 minutes of brisk walking, gentle stretching, or yoga, as tolerated. Avoid heavy lifting or high-impact contact sports if active intracranial pressure is suspected.

## 2. Supportive Remedies & Symptom Relief
- **Fatigue Management**: Post-scan or post-treatment fatigue is common. Plan high-priority activities for morning hours, take short 20-minute power naps, and avoid caffeine after 3 PM.
- **Headache Support**: Use cold compresses or gel packs applied to the forehead or neck for 15 minutes. Practice slow diaphragmatic breathing to ease tension. Always discuss over-the-counter pain medications with your physician.
- **Stress & Anxiety Reduction**: Incorporate 10 minutes of daily guided meditation or mindfulness to lower cortisol levels and promote calm.

## 3. Maintenance Protocols by Scan Result
- **Glioma / Active Tissue Changes**:
  - Maintain a detailed daily symptom and energy log (noting headaches, mood, vision, or energy shifts).
  - Ensure family members and caregivers are trained in basic seizure first aid precautions (roll to the side, keep airways clear, do not place anything in the mouth).
  - Follow anti-inflammatory nutritional support and strict medication adherence.
- **Meningioma (Watchful Waiting & Observation)**:
  - Maintain regular scheduled follow-up MRI scans (typically every 6 to 12 months) to track tumor stability.
  - Monitor for subtle changes in vision, hearing, or focal headaches.
  - Maintain cardiovascular health to promote healthy cerebral circulation.
- **Pituitary Gland Care**:
  - Focus on endocrine balance: maintain balanced blood sugar with low-glycemic meals.
  - Schedule annual or semi-annual visual field checks.
  - Monitor daily energy and hydration balance.
- **Normal / No Tumor Brain Wellness**:
  - Continue proactive preventive brain health with mental exercises (reading, puzzles), balanced diet, and stress management.

## 4. When to Contact Your Doctor Immediately
- Sudden severe headache unlike any previous headache.
- New onset of seizures, muscle spasms, or sudden limb weakness.
- Sudden blurring or loss of peripheral vision.
